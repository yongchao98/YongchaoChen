Yongchao Chen

I am a PHD candidate of Electrical Engineering at Harvard SEAS and MIT LIDS. I am currently working on Human-robot Interaction with Lagre Language Models under the guidance of Prof. Chuchu Fan at MIT. I am also working with Prof. Nicholas Roy at MIT and Dr. Yang Zhang at IBM.

I received my bachelor' degree at University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) with the major of Mechanical Engineering in 2021. Before coming to the Robotics domain, I did researches on Applied Physics, Solid Mechanics, and AI for Science under the guidance of Prof. Ju Li at MIT, Prof. Joost Vlassak at Harvard, and Prof. Ting Zhu at Georgia Tech.

I received the 40th Guo Moruo Award (highest undergraduate honor in USTC) in 2021 and Harvard SEAS PHD Fellowship in 2022.

Research

Human-robot Interaction

I'm currently interested in human-robot interaction and robot planning. Utilizing natural language commands is crucial for mainstream applications of robotics technology. The recent arising of LLMs makes it much more promising.

AutoTAMP: Autoregressive Task and Motion Planning with LLMs as Translators and Checkers
Yongchao Chen, Jacob Arkin, Yang Zhang, Nicholas Roy, Chuchu Fan
arXiv, 2023
Code / Paper

This paper uses LLMs to translate language instructions to formal task specifications that can be solved via a TAMP planner.

NL2TL: Transforming Natural Languages to Temporal Logics using Large Language Models
Yongchao Chen, Rujul Gandhi, Yang Zhang, Chuchu Fan
arXiv, 2023
Project Page / Demo Website / Code / Paper

We propose a framework to achieve accurate and generalizable NL-to-TL transformation with the assistance of LLM, from aspects of both data generation and model training.
